TRUST DEED

This deed of public charitable trust is executed on 3 January 2025 at Sirsi, Uttar Kannada, Karnataka.

I. SETTLOR AND TRUSTEES

SETTLOR:
Mr. Rajesh Hegde, S/o Vinayak Hegde, residing at Marikamba Nagar, Sirsi, Karnataka.

TRUSTEES:
1. Mr. Rajesh Hegde (Managing Trustee)
2. Mrs. Lakshmi Bhat (Trustee)
3. Dr. Kiran Nayak (Trustee)

II. NAME AND OFFICE

The name of the Trust shall be SURAKSHA CHARITABLE TRUST.
Registered office:
Door No. 45, 1st Cross, Marikamba Nagar, Sirsi - 581401, Uttar Kannada District, Karnataka.

III. OBJECTS OF THE TRUST

1. Educational: To establish, maintain, and run schools, colleges, and vocational training centers in rural Karnataka.
2. Medical Relief: To provide medical aid, organize blood donation camps, and offer financial assistance for surgeries to the underprivileged.
3. Environmental: To promote afforestation and conservation of the Western Ghats ecosystem around Sirsi.
4. Relief of Poverty: To provide food and clothing to the needy and victims of natural calamities.

IV. TRUST FUND AND MANAGEMENT

- Initial Fund: INR 10,001 as initial corpus fund.
- Investments: To be made in accordance with Section 11(5) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- Bank Accounts: To be operated jointly by the Managing Trustee and one other Trustee.

V. POWERS OF TRUSTEES

- To accept donations, grants, and legacies from public or government.
- To appoint staff and legal advisors.
- To sell, lease, or mortgage immovable property with prior approval of competent authority.

VI. IRREVOCABILITY AND AMENDMENTS

- Irrevocability: Trust is irrevocable.
- Utilization: Funds to be used only for trust objects; no trustee profit distribution.
- Dissolution: Remaining assets to transfer to another trust with similar objects registered under Section 12A.
